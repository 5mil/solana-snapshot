Step 1 — Load holders

Uses Helius DAS getTokenAccounts — paginated, fast, free-tier friendly. Zero-balance accounts excluded. Decimals fetched from the mint and applied to all amounts.

🔍 Step 2 — Resolve Identities

Instantly labels known CEX/protocol addresses from a built-in table, then optionally looks up .sol domain names via SNS on-chain reverse records. SNS lookup is one RPC call per wallet — use the limit to keep it fast.
